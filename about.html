THE NAME

Why Anāhata?

The name Anāhata is inspired by the language of Nāda, yoga and inner experience.

For Anahata, the distinction between āhata and anāhata becomes a doorway into thinking about sound beyond performance alone — from what can be heard and practiced toward what can be experienced, contemplated and understood.

It is a name that brings together a central thread running through the institution: Yoga ↔ Music ↔ Nāda.
