THE FOUNDER'S ROLE

Building a space
for deeper learning.

Anahata grew from a desire to bring music, traditional knowledge and inquiry into one living space.

As a Carnatic vocalist, teacher and communicator, Ishaan's work moves between structured musical learning, performance, composition, philosophical and traditional studies, and interdisciplinary exploration.

The institution is intentionally larger than any one individual: the goal is to create a space where knowledge can be encountered, respected, practiced and shared across generations.
