THE ANAHATA IDEA

Anahata is more than
a music class.

Anahata is a modern gurukula for Indian music and knowledge.

Rooted in learning, practice, inquiry and shared experience, Anahata brings together learners, musicians, rasikas and seekers across generations and geographies.

Some come to learn music. Some come to explore philosophy and tradition. Some simply come to listen, appreciate and discover.

There is no single way to enter Anahata.
